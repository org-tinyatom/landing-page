Spreadsheet to app: common questions

What TinyAtom does with spreadsheet workflows, including what it does not automate.

Can I turn a spreadsheet into an app?

Yes. Use the spreadsheet as the source material for the tool: describe its columns, formulas, decisions, and the steps people perform around it. In TinyAtom, your coding assistant builds an interface and workflow around those rules, then you review and install the result as a local app.

Does TinyAtom automatically convert every spreadsheet?

TinyAtom is not a one-click spreadsheet converter. It helps you build the tool the spreadsheet was standing in for. That lets the result match the real workflow instead of reproducing every cell and tab.

Can I build an app from Excel or CSV data?

Yes. Excel and CSV files can be source material for a TinyAtom tool. Explain how the team uses the data and what the app should let people review, change, calculate, or export.

Where does the spreadsheet app store its data?

A TinyAtom tool stores its data on the computer where it runs. Before it can open a spreadsheet or another file, TinyAtom checks that the tool requested access and that you allowed it.
